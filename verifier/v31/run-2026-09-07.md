## Diagnosis

The four "Put {service} on a documented schedule" sections were not a shared
component — they were one inline block inside the dynamic template
`app/services/[service]/page.tsx` with two variants: a photography-backed
forest band for landscaping, and a bg-tint band for the other three services.
Removing that single block (79 lines, comment through closing conditional)
removes all four sections at once. `ArrowRightIcon` import retained (still
used elsewhere in the template).

## Change (1 file)

- app/services/[service]/page.tsx — entire proposal-CTA block deleted
  (both variants: heading, description, "Request a proposal for …" button,
  "All services" button, tint/photo backgrounds, wrappers, padding).

Spacing after removal required no new code: the sections that are now last
carry their own bottom padding — FAQ `py-16 md:py-24` (64/96px) on
landscaping + asphalt; Related projects `py-16 md:py-20` (64/80px) on snow +
hardscape — inside the brief's 64–96px target. No leftover containers,
spacers, or margins: the block was a single self-closing conditional.

Not touched (per brief): Request a Proposal page/nav/footer links, service
content, images, videos, portfolio, footer, header. The phrase "documented
schedule" survives once in hardscape body copy (maintenance bullet) — that is
service content, not a CTA.
