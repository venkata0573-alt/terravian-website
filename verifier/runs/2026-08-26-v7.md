Trigger: live-site feedback — phone hero badly cropped, too much written
content, structure/alignment poor on mobile. Desktop untouched.

## Changes

1. **Hero (HeroRotator.tsx)** — phones (<768px) now get dedicated
   art-directed 828×1472 portrait crops (`*-mobile.avif`, new files; masters
   untouched) served via `<picture><source media>`, so the subject (flowering
   tree/entrance, loader fleet, fire pit) fills the frame instead of a
   center-slice of the wide master. Mobile gains a visible headline block
   (desktop stays headline-free per earlier client direction): snug
   sharp-cornered charcoal/70 panel with gold eyebrow
   (Commercial Property Care — Cheshire, CT), visible tagline headline
   (aria-hidden; single sr-only h1 kept), one-line sub, and two full-width
   thumb-friendly CTAs; slide dots moved under the panel. Hero height
   78svh/min-600 mobile, 70svh desktop unchanged.
2. **DivisionCards** — mobile cards now 4/5 portrait (was 4/3): photography
   dominates and title/body/CTA no longer crowd each other.
3. **SnowCommand** — mobile becomes compact horizontal rows (number rail +
   copy, tighter type); the 8 tall stacked cards (~2400px wall) collapse to
   ~700px. sm+ grid unchanged.
4. **MaintenanceCalendar** — sticky division column while scrolling
   sideways + "Swipe sideways for the full year →" cue on mobile.
5. **TestimonialPlaceholders** — mobile becomes a snap-x swipe row (78%
   cards) instead of three tall stacked boxes that read as broken.

## Notable diagnostic finding

Earlier "giant blank regions" in full-page screenshots were a capture
artifact: the site sets `scroll-smooth` globally, so programmatic scrolls
animate; screenshots taken mid-flight showed Reveal (whileInView) sections
still at opacity 0. Re-audited with `reducedMotion: 'reduce'` (animations
instant) — no real blank regions exist. Footer logo "white box" was the
same artifact (intentional white chip, logo loads fine).
