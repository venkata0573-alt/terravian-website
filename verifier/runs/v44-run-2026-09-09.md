Root causes found in audit:
- Loader: source PNG carried 1,345 semi-transparent pixels (alpha 1–3) forming a
  faint square ring outside the emblem, and the drop-shadow filter sat on the
  ROTATING element, exposing rectangular raster bounds during rotation.
- Hero carousel mounted all 4 slides' <picture> at t=0 → slides 2–4 downloaded
  immediately despite opacity-0.
- Below-fold marquees (FieldMarquee, TrustPlaceholders) were loading="eager".
- Card/thumbnail slots (300–680px) referenced 1400–4288px masters with no srcset.
- Several masters were heavier than their display role required.

## Evaluation harness (/tmp/lab/evaluate.js)

Checks: loader asset ≤30 KB; per-page initial-load byte budgets; carousel slides
3–4 zero bytes at load; first hero present and crisp @1920 (served width ≥ slot×DPR);
zero broken images after full-page scroll on 13 routes; homepage CLS ≤ 0.05.

Criterion revision (documented during the run): the re-encode guard started as
PSNR-based, but lossy→lossy re-encoding caps PSNR at ~33–38 dB regardless of quality
(q90 roundtrip measured 45.7 dB), so PSNR was rejecting visually lossless candidates.
Guard revised to SSIM ≥ 0.97 (skimage) + ≥35% size reduction, quality ladder
q62/70/75/80/85 first pass wins. Every shipped recompression passed the revised guard;
anything failing was left untouched.
