# v11 addendum — 2026-08-31 — footer brand lockup fix

## Complaint
Logo plate + tagline did not read as one composed brand unit in the footer.

## Root cause (measured, not guessed)
The tagline inherited the grid column's full width (300-350px) and was
left-aligned; the 128px plate is ~3x narrower. Pixel measurement of the
rendered footer: plate center vs tagline-text center offset = +58.5px at
1440/768/390. An intermediate attempt (container max-w-[240px], centered,
18px one line) still left a +37.5px offset — verified insufficient by
pixel measurement before shipping.

## Fix (container relationship, not arbitrary nudges)
components/layout/SiteFooter.tsx: brand cell is now a fixed `w-36` (144px)
unit — 16px wider than the 128px plate. The tagline (18px italic display)
wraps to two lines and text-centers inside that unit, so line centers sit
within ±8-10px of the plate center at every breakpoint. mt-5 → mt-4.

## Rendered verification (Playwright + pixel analysis)
- Offsets: +58.5px → +10.0px at 1440, 768, AND 390 (tagline half-widths
  ~69px vs plate half-width 64px — optically centered).
- Zoomed crops inspected at each step; two-line composition reads as one
  lockup. Full-footer balance at 1440/768/390 confirmed unchanged elsewhere.
- Gates: tsc clean; 27/27 build; 0 overflow at 1440/390/360; 0 broken
  images; /, /industries, /services, /request-a-proposal → 200, overflow 0.
